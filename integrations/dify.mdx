# Integrating PhotoMem with Dify AI

PhotoMem brings a robust memory layer to Dify AI, empowering your AI agents with persistent conversation storage and retrieval capabilities. With PhotoMem, your Dify applications gain the ability to recall past interactions and maintain context, ensuring more natural and insightful conversations.

---

## How to Integrate PhotoMem in Your Dify Workflow

1. **Install the PhotoMem Plugin:**  
   Head to the [Dify Marketplace](https://marketplace.dify.ai/plugins/yevanchen/photomem) and install the PhotoMem plugin. This is your first step toward adding intelligent memory to your AI applications.

2. **Create or Open Your Dify Project:**  
   Whether you're starting fresh or updating an existing project, simply create or open your Dify workspace.

3. **Add the PhotoMem Plugin to Your Project:**  
   Within your project, add the PhotoMem plugin. This integration connects PhotoMem’s memory management capabilities directly to your Dify application.

4. **Configure Your PhotoMem Settings:**  
   Customize PhotoMem to suit your needs—set preferences for how conversation history is stored, the search parameters, and any other context-aware features.

5. **Leverage PhotoMem in Your Workflow:**  
   Use PhotoMem to store every conversation turn and retrieve past interactions seamlessly. This integration ensures that your AI agents can refer back to important context, making multi-turn dialogues more effective and user-centric.
